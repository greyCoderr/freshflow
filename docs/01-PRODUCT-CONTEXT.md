# FreshFlow Product Context

FreshFlow is a proposal-stage web application for BFDC. It has one customer-facing website and one protected staff/admin workspace connected to the same products, orders, prices, and inventory records.

## Primary users

### Customer

Needs to browse products, understand availability, manage a cart, place an order, track its recorded status, and submit feedback or concerns without learning internal BFDC processes.

### Staff

Needs to process orders, complete walk-in POS sales, monitor product stock, record legitimate movements, and respond to customer concerns quickly and accurately.

### Administrator

Needs all authorized operational functions plus account, role, permission, product, and audit management.

## Main product areas

- Public/customer product catalog
- Product details
- Cart and checkout
- Customer orders and order details
- POS
- Staff order management
- Inventory balances
- Stock movement history
- Feedback and concern management
- User, role, and permission management
- Notifications and audit history

## Product principles

- Simple and spacious rather than information-heavy
- Clear next action on every screen
- Shared source of truth for products, prices, sales, orders, and inventory
- Status text accompanied by color, never color alone
- Prevention of mistakes before correction
- Historical records retained for accountability
- Customer convenience without exposing internal operations

## Scope exclusions

- Supplier application and approval
- Payroll and general accounting
- Online card or wallet payment
- Real-time vehicle GPS
- Physical production automation
- External marketplace integration

These exclusions remain in force unless the project owner explicitly changes the validated objectives.
